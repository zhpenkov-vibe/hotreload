Update: ARTEX-driven attack disrupts the European Commission

The open-source ARTEX AI hacking framework flagged by the FBI and AWS earlier this week was used in a campaign that disrupted the European Commission's internal systems, which the Commission admitted on Nov 19; Amazon's researchers reportedly traced it to unchanged passwords and outdated software. Nvidia stops selling H200 chips in China

Acting CEO Colette Kress said on Nvidia's first earnings call since Jensen Huang stepped down that it is no longer shipping licensed Hopper data-centre products to China, ending the arrangement that sent chips via the US under a 25% tariff. Shifts in who can buy frontier compute ripple into the price and availability of the AI design and coding tools your team depends on.

Reuters: Meta's Muse agent pulled private chats in internal tests

A Reuters investigation published Nov 18 found Meta testers warning that the Muse agent could read Signal message content through Android notification access, mix up conversations and surface sensitive data, which Meta called isolated incidents.
