Figma adds native 3D transforms to the canvas

Figma launched a 3D transform effect on Nov 12 that rotates layers on the x, y and z axes with on-canvas handles, in open beta for Full seats, and its agent can build 3D designs too. If designers start shipping tilted cards and perspective hero art, your component library will need matching transform and perspective tokens before those mocks reach code.

Google ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen as its fast everyday model

Announced Nov 12, Hydrogen is Google's speed-focused model for coding and agent workflows, which Google says runs 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash with far fewer fabricated claims, at a reported $1.50 in and $7.50 out per million tokens. It is in AI Studio, the Gemini API and Android Studio now, so it is a cheap candidate for high-volume jobs like generating component docs or story variants.
