Update: Storybook 11 reaches beta 5 as the RC date arrives

Storybook shipped 11.0.0-beta.5 on Nov 23, the day before its planned Nov 24 release-candidate target, with stable still aimed at Dec 1. If you plan to upgrade your component library this year, run the beta on a branch now and note that Controls still need TypeScript 5.9 or 6.x until TypeScript 7.1 is stable.

UK NCSC publishes its assessment of AI and cyber intrusion

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre released a November assessment of how AI is changing intrusions through 2028, judging that AI-assisted vulnerability discovery and exploit development will shrink the time defenders have to patch. Teams with slow upgrade cycles, including design-system packages pinned to old dependencies, are the ones this squeezes most.

Read source →

GlobeTopper breach exposes customers' API keys

A cyberattack on gift-card platform GlobeTopper, reported Nov 23, cost an estimated $2.5 million and gave the intruder API credentials and integration keys for some business customers. Any integration keys your docs, demo apps or Storybook deploys depend on should be scoped narrowly and easy to rotate in minutes.
