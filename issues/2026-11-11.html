GitHub will switch off Dependabot alerts on idle repos from Dec 8

Repositories with no activity in the past year lose Dependabot alerts on Dec 8; existing alerts are archived, and any push or re-enabling restores them. Idle or deprecated component packages in your design-system org will go quiet, so list them now and either keep them active or archive them on purpose.
