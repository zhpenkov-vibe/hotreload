Anthropic moves its Messages API from Python to Rust, with Claude doing much of the work

Anthropic wrote on Nov 25 that the Rust rewrite cut CPU per request by about 70% and memory by more than 80%, with median time to first token about 20% lower, and that Claude agents wrote much of the new code. The useful lesson for library maintainers is the verification story: comparing old and new behaviour on real traffic caught differences the ported tests missed, including weakened assertions.

OpenAI starts testing Private Inference for business prompts

Sam Altman announced on Nov 25 an alpha, for a small group of business customers, that runs inference in hardware-isolated environments so prompts and responses stay hidden while they are processed. If your team keeps unreleased product designs or customer data away from AI tools, this is a sign that confidential inference may soon be something you can ask vendors for; models, pricing and regions are not yet disclosed.
