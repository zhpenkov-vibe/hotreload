Chrome 158 beta adds <install> and media-state pseudo-classes

The beta brings an <install> element for installing a web app without JavaScript, :playing, :paused and :muted pseudo-classes, and flow-start/flow-end aliases for Grid Lanes, with stable due Nov 17. Media pseudo-classes let a player component style itself from real state instead of JS-toggled classes, so try your media primitives against the beta now.
